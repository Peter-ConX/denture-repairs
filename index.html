What We Do

Repairs & services
we can help with

From a single broken tooth to a cracked base plate or ill-fitting partial — we handle a wide range of denture repair work.

Broken or Missing Teeth

Replacement of individual teeth that have come loose, cracked, or broken off — on full or partial dentures.

Cracked or Split Base

Repair of hairline cracks, splits, or full breaks in the denture plate — whether acrylic or partial base.

Chrome Partial Frameworks

Repairs to metal partial dentures including broken clasps, framework adjustments, and tooth additions.

Relines

When your dentures have become loose over time, a reline can improve their fit against the gum surface.

Clasp Repairs

Replacement or adjustment of wire and cast clasps on partial dentures that have snapped or no longer grip.

Cleaning & Polish

Professional cleaning and polishing to remove staining, tartar, and surface marks — restoring a fresh appearance.

Not sure if we can help? Just ask — we're happy to advise.

Common questions

We work on most types of denture including full acrylic dentures, acrylic partial dentures, and chrome (cobalt-chrome) partial frameworks. If you're unsure, just describe what you have when you call or message us.

It depends on the repair needed. Simple repairs can often be done more quickly than complex framework work. We'll give you an honest timeframe when we see your dentures in person.
